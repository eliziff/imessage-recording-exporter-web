        Info
        
The exporter samples the recording, matches overlapping conversation frames, removes repeated areas, and joins the remaining content into one sequence. PDF pages and numbered images are created from that sequence.

        
Large jumps with no visible overlap can create gaps. Animated media, rapid scrolling, overlays, and content that leaves the recorded screen can reduce reconstruction accuracy. Video clips retain the full recorded screen view so scrolling and partial visibility are preserved without inventing missing frames.
